# Visual reference library: post-press finishing

Gathered 2026-10-17.

## Read this first: what this library can and cannot claim
- **No image was viewed.** Pages were read as text only: the Chrome extension was not connected, and the brief forbids downloading images. "Lighting", "reflection" and "edge" fields are filled **only** when the page's own text describes them. Otherwise the field says `not described`. The field `Confidence` means **confidence that the page shows a real, physical sample of that process**, not confidence about the visual details.
- Stock photos, AI images (for example the krea.ai and uxpilot "gold leaf" generator results that came up in search) and pure renders were left out.
- **Next step (Phase 1b, manual).** A human should open each URL, confirm it is a real photo, and fill in camera angle, light direction and reflection behaviour. Better still, photograph physical swatches under the app's own overhead soft-key + fill setup. Only the tog.ink reference (A1-1) describes overhead lighting behaviour in words.

Legend: Real = real physical sample photographed; Sim = render or mockup; ? = not stated.

### Corrections to the text-only research from the inspected photos
1. Metallic foils (gold/silver/coloured) show a **paper-relief glint texture** (fine sparkle) and a
   bright→dark gradient; the renderer's metal model needs a substrate-following micro-normal term.
2. **먹박 is usually gloss black** on matte stock (visible as sheen contrast), not a matte pigment.
   The catalogue now defaults 먹박 to gloss black and keeps a separate matte black pigment foil.
3. Holographic foil under soft light reads as **pale pastel iridescence**, confirming "weak rainbow
   unless the light is directional".
4. Raised UV is clearly distinct from spot UV in real samples (visible thickness, bubbles).
5. Domes: bright rim + dark edge outline + one dominant highlight band; print magnified.
