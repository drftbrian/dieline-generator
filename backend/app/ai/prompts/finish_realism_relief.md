- Embossing / debossing pressed into paperboard: the shapes are physically raised (emboss) or
  sunken (deboss) by a fraction of a millimetre.
- Soft light rakes gently across the relief: one flank catches a little light, the opposite flank
  a soft shadow; rounded shoulders; the paper fibre stretches slightly over the edges.
- Subtle and real, never a drop shadow, glow or outline.
