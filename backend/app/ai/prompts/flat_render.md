## 2. REDLINE INTERPRETATION — CRITICAL

The Figma file uses the same red colour for both folding lines and cutting lines. They are differentiated by
position, and this has already been resolved in Image 1:

**OUTER REDLINE (the outermost closed outline of the sheet) = CUTTING LINE.** This is the die-cut edge of the
paperboard. Everything outside it is the black background.

**REDLINES INSIDE THE OUTLINE = FOLDING LINES.** Render them as subtle physical crease impressions while keeping
the cardboard continuous.

**CLOSED REDLINE LOOPS INSIDE THE OUTLINE (windows, holes, slots) = CUTTING LINES.** These are genuine openings;
the black background shows through them.

Do not classify redlines by colour alone, and do not override this convention with assumptions based on
conventional packaging standards. Follow Image 1 exactly.
